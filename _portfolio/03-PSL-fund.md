I am delighted to share that our ***CRISP*** project has been selected for funding through the [2026 PSL Global Seed Fund](https://psl.eu/en/news/international-call-proposals-psl-global-seed-fund-spring-2026)! 🌊

Our proposal was one of 17 projects selected from 33 submissions and has been awarded **€8,000** to launch this new international collaboration with our Swedish partner, **Stockholm Resilience Centre (Stockholm University)**.

**CRISP: Climate-Ready High-Seas Marine Protected Areas under Climate Change and Fishing Pressure  **will ask a simple but urgent question:****

**Where should new Marine Protected Areas be established on the high seas so that they remain effective in a rapidly changing ocean?**

With the BBNJ Agreement now in force (Jan 2026), the international community has a new legal framework for establishing Marine Protected Areas beyond national jurisdiction. However, effective protection requires more than drawing static boundaries on a map. It requires understanding how climate change and fishing pressure interact across space and over time.

CRISP will combine global observations of compound ocean climate change, fishing-activity data, and future climate projections to:

🔹 identify regions exposed to both intense climate and fishing pressures;
🔹 locate potential climate refuges under the climate-ready MPAs framework;
🔹 distinguish “high-gain but hard-to-win” conservation opportunities from more feasible “easy-win” areas
🔹 translate this evidence into actionable guidance on where high-seas protected areas should be created, what they should protect, and how they should be managed.

I will serve as the project PI on the PSL side, working closely with **Robert Blasiak** at the **Stockholm Resilience Centre, Stockholm University**, together with **Joachim Claudet** at EPHE–PSL/CNRS and **Sabrina Speich** and **Laurent Bopp** at ENS–PSL/LMD.

By bringing together ocean observation, climate science, marine ecology, conservation, fisheries, and ocean governance, we hope to build a genuinely interdisciplinary bridge from scientific evidence to international decision-making.

The project will support research exchanges between France and Sweden.

**The CRISP execution period: October 2026 - September 2027, with a total budget of 8,000 euros.**

Many thanks to the entire team and to Université PSL for supporting this collaboration. I am very much looking forward to getting ***CRISP*** underway!
