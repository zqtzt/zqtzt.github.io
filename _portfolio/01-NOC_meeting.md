From September 21 to September 23, 2026, I had the opportunity to have a short-visit at the [National Oceanography Centre (NOC)](https://www.noc.ac.uk/) in the Southampton, UK.

 During the visit, I had productive discussions with Prof. Elaine McDonagh about regional changes in water masses, and we identified several priorities for the next stage of the research. I also had the pleasure of delivering on the **Physical Oceanography Seminar** to colleagues at NOC with the title “***Compound Ocean State Change: Observational Evidences and Physical Mechanism***”, with follow-up many interesting dicussions.
