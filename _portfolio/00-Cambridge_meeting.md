From September 15 to September 18, 2026, I joined [OCEAN ICE](https://ocean-ice.eu/) final annual meeting at the **British Antarctic Survey** in Cambridge. 

🎉 I was very glad to present and share our latest progress of the **variability of the South Atlantic Antarctic Intermediate Water (AAIW),** as well as the **new ocean observations deployment and recovery of the SAMBA line (34.5S)**. I also exchanged ideas with colleagues across the project, and discuss how some of these questions can be taken forward beyond OCEAN ICE.

🎇  As OCEAN ICE approaches its conclusion this October, the meeting was also a good opportunity to look back at the scientific questions that have gradually shaped my research over the past few years. A central theme of my work in this project has been to **understand how the Antarctic Intermediate Water (AAIW) are transmitted through the ocean circulation, and how these changes propagate into the South Atlantic and influenced by the Indian Ocean.**

🎇 When I joined the OCEAN ICE two years ago, I had far more questions than answers about where this research would lead. Looking back now, these ideas have developed into a much clearer scientific story, **including two manuscripts currently in submission (Tan et al., 2026a, 2026b).**

🌐 Going forward, I hope to push this work further by moving from detecting and understanding the mechanisms of regional ocean state changes from the South Atlantic to a wider scale, and connecting these physical changes to broader ocean climate risks.

It was also a great opportunity to reconnect with old friends, meet new colleagues working across Antarctic and the Southern Ocean science.
